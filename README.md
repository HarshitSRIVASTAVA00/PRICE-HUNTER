# 🛍️ Smart Price Hunter

> **AI-Powered E-commerce Price Intelligence Platform**

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-Demo-brightgreen?style=for-the-badge&logo=githubpages)](https://harshitsrivastava00.github.io/PRICE-HUNTER/)


**Smart Price Hunter** is an advanced e-commerce price intelligence and comparison web application designed to help users track, compare, and discover the best deals across multiple online stores. Powered by simulated AI agents, it scans the e-commerce landscape to bring you real-time price comparisons, deal scores, and maximum savings analysis.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🤖 AI Agents Integration:** Simulates intelligent agents scanning multiple e-commerce platforms in real-time.
- **📊 Comprehensive Price Comparison:** Compare product prices across different online retailers instantly.
- **🎯 Smart Deal Scoring:** Proprietary deal score distribution algorithm to highlight the absolute best value-for-money products.
- **🔍 Advanced Filtering & Sorting:**
  - Sort by Price (Low to High / High to Low)
  - Filter by Highest Rated & Best Discounts
  - Filter by Deal Score & Maximum Savings
  - Site-specific filtering and custom Price Range sliders (₹0 - ₹200,000+)
- **📈 Interactive Analytics:** Built-in charts displaying price comparisons by site and deal score distributions.
- **⚡ Modern UI/UX:** Clean, responsive, and intuitive dashboard built for seamless desktop and mobile experiences.
